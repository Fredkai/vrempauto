We offer standard shipping (3-7 business days) and expedited shipping (1-3 business days) for spare parts. Free shipping is available for orders over $500. International shipping is available with customs duties being the customer's responsibility.

Unused and uninstalled parts can be returned within 7 days of delivery in original packaging. Custom orders and electrical components are non-returnable. Contact our support team for a Return Authorization number before shipping returns.

Yes, all parts come with manufacturer warranty coverage. Warranty periods vary by product and manufacturer. Warranty claims require proof of purchase and must be made within the warranty period. Contact us for specific warranty information on any part.
